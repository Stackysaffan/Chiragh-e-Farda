01 · About Us

A lamp lit for tomorrow

Chirag-e-Farda — "Lamp of Tomorrow" — was founded in 2026 in Lahore on a belief as old as the city's own lanterns: no child's future should stay dim because of the street they were born on. We work directly inside underserved communities to open the door to education, and to keep it open.

Our Mission

The world around us is changing every day. Technology is evolving, new fields are emerging, and the careers of tomorrow may be ones we cannot even imagine today. But for many of our children, these possibilities remain distant and unknown. All children deserve more than just an education. They deserve the opportunity to imagine a future for themselves regardless of the circumstances surrounding them. Chiragh-e-Farda is a student-led initiative built on a simple belief: A child’s circumstances should never limit the future they can dream of. We aim to introduce children to the world beyond their immediate surroundings — to science, technology, creativity, different careers, and new ways of thinking. Through interactive sessions, activities, experiments, mentorship, and educational resources, we hope to spark something far more important than knowledge: Curiosity. Because sometimes, all it takes is a glimpse of what is possible to change the way a child sees their future

Hope

We treat hope as something we deliver, not just a feeling we hold — every program starts from what's possible.

Access

We remove the everyday barriers — cost, distance, documentation — that keep children out of the classroom.

Dignity

Every family we work with is a partner, not a project. We listen first, and build alongside communities.

06 · FAQs

Good to know

What does Chirag-e-Farda actually do?+

We work directly with underserved communities in Lahore to get children into school and keep them there — through direct support, community partnerships, and awareness work.

How can I help if I don't live in Lahore?+

You can donate, spread awareness, or partner with us remotely — many of our supporters are based outside Lahore and even outside Pakistan.

Is my donation tax-deductible?+

We're finalising our registration and tax-credit status — this section will be updated with confirmed details soon. Contact us directly for the current status.

Reach out through the contact form or email address above and mark your enquiry as "Media enquiry" — we aim to respond within a few working days.
